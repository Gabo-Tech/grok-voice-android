First launch

Five setup paths

The tour asks for the screen language, then one path. Finish later keeps the step. Settings, then Voice and AI, opens the same path again. The setup guide lists where each key comes from and what to do when it fails.

  1. On this phone No key. Moonshine hears you, a local model thinks, and Phonon or Piper speaks.
  2. xAI A Grok key. Hearing, thinking, and the voice run in the cloud.
  3. OpenAI An OpenAI key. Hearing, thinking, and the voice run in the cloud.
  4. 1min.ai A 1min.ai key writes the reply. Hearing and the voice stay on this phone.
  5. Phone, then a cloud key This phone answers first. A saved cloud key is used only if the phone cannot.

Offline packs

Weights stay out of the APK.

The first download is English: Moonshine, SmolLM2 135M Instruct Q8_0, and Phonon 31m. Together that is 460,669,758 bytes. llama.cpp b12774 runs the GGUF, including Qwen3.5 and Gemma. Piper is the other on-device voice. Nothing else downloads until you tap Download.

Optional chat models are Qwen3.5 0.8B, 2B, and 4B, Gemma 3 1B, Gemma 4 E2B, Llama 3.2 1B, Qwen2.5 0.5B, and LFM2.5 350M. The phone recommends the strongest model that fits. Spanish and German speech packs, and gated Phonon 40m voices, are added later in Voice and AI. Sizes and checksums are in the developer notes.

On the phone

Talk uses the same records as the screen.

  • Talk Tap the control, or say “Hey Grok” when Listen is on. What you say sits on the right. Grok’s reply sits on the left. The wake word is trained on the phone from three recordings. Those samples are not uploaded.
  • Time Reminders, alarms, and countdowns each have a list, a search, and an add button. A repeat can be every N days, weeks, months, or years. Cancelling asks for a yes.
  • Calendar Talk saves new events on this app’s calendar. Reading the phone calendar is separate. An event added on the Calendar screen, with access granted, can still go to the phone calendar.
  • People Nicknames, a preferred channel, and dates live in this app. Phone numbers and email are read from Android contacts and are not written back. A call or a message waits for a clear yes.
  • Finance Overview, Activity, Accounts, Holdings, and Plan stay on the phone. There is no bank login. Activity keeps transactions and repeating bills apart, and filters apply to both. A card charge counts as spending. Paying a card or a loan does not. What you still owe is listed on its own. Plan is a budget for one month. Holdings shares add up to 100%. You can ask Talk to add, check, change, or remove a budget, and a delete waits until you say yes. A color you pick for a holding, account, or category is saved on the phone and used wherever that item appears.
  • Notes and music Notes are saved on the phone. Music uses the default player in Settings. Saying “on YouTube” still waits for a yes, and the app does not download that video.
  • Widgets and themes Home-screen widgets: Listen, Reminders, Coming up, Net worth, This month, Next 30 days, and Next. They follow the theme. Themes are Graphite, Ink, Stone, Midnight, Carbon, Ivory, Sand, Forest, Plum, System, and Custom.
  • Five languages Screen copy is English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, and Valencian (en, es, de, pt, ca-ES-valencia). The first offline voice stays English until you add another pack.

Voice commands, with an example in each language. The same list, written as speech in one language at a time: English, Español, Deutsch, Português, Valencià.

Privacy

No server of its own.

The app does not collect analytics. A cloud key, if you add one, stays in the on-device vault. An xAI key is sent to api.x.ai only to mint a short-lived voice token, and only while xAI is the provider in use. OpenAI works the same way with its own key. 1min.ai sees the text of the reply path. Hearing and the voice for that path stay on the phone. A Hugging Face token, if you add one, is used only to download a gated Phonon model.

The install guide covers sideloading the APK. Troubleshooting covers a refused key, a missing pack, and a microphone that stops.

How do I install it?
Download the arm64 APK and open it on the phone, or run adb install -r GrokVoice-0.1.65.apk. Android 8 (API 26) or newer. It is not a Play Store upload. The release is signed with the sideload certificate so an update installs over an existing copy.
Does Talk invent money?
No. Balances are what you saved. A missing exchange rate is refused. Combined totals do not treat a missing rate as one-to-one.
