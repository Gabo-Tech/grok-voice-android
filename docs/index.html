Talk, or type

Wake word

Listen stays on after you leave. Nothing starts until you say “Hey Grok.”

Quiet gap

After a pause you choose, from 5 to 120 seconds, the conversation ends and only the wake word remains.

A page for notes

Titles, search, and Markdown. Preview headings, lists, and links before you save.

On the phone

  • NotesSave, retitle, and search what you asked Grok to remember.
  • RemindersOne-off alerts, alarms, and timers.
  • CalendarToday, and a month you can move through. If the phone calendar is unavailable, events stay in the app.
  • SearchNews, weather, and what is on in town, spoken and written, with sources.

The key stays here

You paste a key during setup. The app checks it, then locks it in on-device storage. Voice uses a short-lived token minted on the phone. Wake-word recordings are not uploaded.

You can keep more than one key, in the order you prefer. If the first connection fails, the next is tried. Spoken replies use xAI realtime voice today.
