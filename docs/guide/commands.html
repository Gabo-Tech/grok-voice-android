Commands

What Talk can do.

Each block is one tool from actions::tool_definitions, finance::tool_definitions, or, where marked, the on-device memory tools. Say it in your own words. The line in each language is one example, not a required script. ca is ca-ES-valencia. A block marked “waits for yes” calls the tool first with confirmed false.

The same material as continuous speech, one language per page: English, Español, Deutsch, Português, Valencià.

Notes

create_note

Save a note on this phone. The title is a short name. The text may be Markdown.

  • en Make a note: buy milk.
  • es Crea una nota: comprar leche.
  • de Mach eine Notiz: Milch kaufen.
  • pt Crie uma nota: comprar leite.
  • ca Crea una nota: compra llet.

list_notes

Read or search notes saved on this phone. A query matches the title or the text. An id returns one note.

  • en What notes do I have about milk?
  • es ¿Qué notas tengo sobre la leche?
  • de Welche Notizen habe ich zu Milch?
  • pt Que notas eu tenho sobre leite?
  • ca Quines notes tinc sobre la llet?

update_note

Change a note. The id comes from list_notes. Pass a title, text, or both.

  • en Change that note.
  • es Cambia esa nota.
  • de Ändere diese Notiz.
  • pt Mude essa nota.
  • ca Canvia eixa nota.

delete_note

Delete one saved note by id from list_notes. This tool does not take a confirmed flag.

  • en Delete the milk note.
  • es Borra la nota de la leche.
  • de Lösche die Milch-Notiz.
  • pt Apague a nota do leite.
  • ca Esborra la nota de la llet.

Time

set_alarm

Save an alarm on this app’s Time list for the next time that hour and minute occurs. A delay such as in 5 minutes is a reminder or a countdown, not an alarm. A YouTube link or a sound file already picked in the form can be the sound. The app does not download the video. Recurrence can be daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or every N days, weeks, months, or years. endCount or until can stop it. delivery is spoken or normal. Omit delivery to follow Settings.

  • en Set an alarm for 7:30.
  • es Pon una alarma a las 7:30.
  • de Stelle einen Wecker auf 7:30.
  • pt Coloque um alarme para 7:30.
  • ca Posa una alarma a les 7:30.

set_timer

Start a countdown on Time. seconds, inMinutes, or inHours is the length. A label is what it is for. A request to remind you uses set_reminder instead.

  • en Set a timer for 10 minutes.
  • es Pon un temporizador de 10 minutos.
  • de Stelle einen Timer auf 10 Minuten.
  • pt Coloque um timer de 10 minutos.
  • ca Posa un compte enrere de 10 minuts.

set_reminder

Save a reminder on Time. inMinutes or inHours is a delay, and the app computes the fire time. A clock time uses at. Repeats use the same recurrence fields as an alarm.

  • en Remind me in 5 minutes to check the oven.
  • es Recuérdame en 5 minutos que mire el horno.
  • de Erinnere mich in 5 Minuten, den Ofen zu prüfen.
  • pt Lembre-me em 5 minutos de ver o forno.
  • ca Recorda’m d’ací a 5 minuts que mire el forn.

snooze_reminder

Snooze a reminder by minutes or until a clock time. With no id, it snoozes the one that is ringing. If the result says to ask, it asks which one.

  • en Snooze that 10 minutes.
  • es Pospón eso 10 minutos.
  • de Schlummere das 10 Minuten.
  • pt Adie isso 10 minutos.
  • ca Ajorna-ho 10 minuts.

list_reminders

List reminders, alarms, and timers, including repeats. Person dates appear only inside their notify window.

  • en What reminders do I have?
  • es ¿Qué recordatorios tengo?
  • de Welche Erinnerungen habe ich?
  • pt Que lembretes eu tenho?
  • ca Quins recordatoris tinc?

update_reminder

Change a reminder’s title, note, time, repeat, or an alarm sound. Custom sound is only for alarms.

  • en Move my reminder to 5.
  • es Mueve mi recordatorio a las 5.
  • de Verschiebe meine Erinnerung auf 5.
  • pt Mude o meu lembrete para as 5.
  • ca Mou el meu recordatori a les 5.

cancel_reminder

Cancel one reminder, alarm, or countdown. The first call uses confirmed false and does not delete. Say the title, wait for yes, then call again with confirmed true. Waits for a clear yes.

  • en Cancel the oven reminder.
  • es Cancela el recordatorio del horno.
  • de Brich die Ofen-Erinnerung ab.
  • pt Cancele o lembrete do forno.
  • ca Cancel·la el recordatori del forn.

List events for a day, week, month, or range in one call. Recurring events expand into each occurrence. Omit every date argument for today.

  • pt O que tenho na agenda hoje?
  • ca Què tinc hui al calendari?

create_calendar_event

Save an event on this app’s Calendar. start and end are local. If end is omitted, it lasts one hour. Recurrence is daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly.

  • de Trag ein Mittagessen mit Ana morgen um 1 ein.
  • pt Adicione um almoço com a Ana amanhã à 1.
  • ca Afig un dinar amb Ana demà a la 1.

update_calendar_event

Change a calendar event. The id comes from list_calendar_events. Pass only the fields that change.

  • en Move lunch to 2.
  • es Mueve la comida a las 2.
  • de Verschiebe das Mittagessen auf 2.
  • pt Mude o almoço para as 2.
  • ca Mou el dinar a les 2.

delete_calendar_event

Delete a calendar event by id from list_calendar_events. This tool does not take a confirmed flag.

  • en Delete the lunch event.
  • es Borra el evento de la comida.
  • de Lösche den Mittagessen-Termin.
  • pt Apague o evento do almoço.

update_person

Change a saved name, note, channel, or date. It does not write the phone address book.

delete_person

Delete one saved person. Say who, call first with confirmed false, then again after a yes. The phone address book is unchanged. Waits for a clear yes.

Finance

log_money

Log an expense, income, or a payment toward a card or debt. Logging never creates an account or a card. Several expenses can be one request. A correction in the same sentence saves only the corrected amount. Charging a card is an expense and does not move the bank. Paying a card is not an expense. A missing exchange rate is refused.

  • en I spent 12 euros on coffee.
  • es He gastado 12 euros en café.
  • de Ich habe 12 Euro für Kaffee ausgegeben.
  • pt Gastei 12 euros no café.
  • ca He gastat 12 euros en café.

save_account

Save or update a bank, cash wallet, card, loan, other debt, or investment you described. A new one is created only when no saved name matches. Never store a full card number, account number, or PIN. Last four only. color is a hex color on that nickname.

  • en Add a bank called Checking with 500 euros.
  • es Añade un banco llamado Checking con 500 euros.
  • de Füge eine Bank namens Checking mit 500 Euro hinzu.
  • pt Adicione um banco chamado Checking com 500 euros.
  • ca Afig un banc que es diu Checking amb 500 euros.

list_finance

Read what is saved: summary, accounts, activity, the next 30 days, or investments. It does not guess amounts or rates. refresh true fetches today’s rates and holding prices. A color question reads, sets, or clears one saved color and does not create an account.

  • en What’s my net worth?
  • es ¿Cuál es mi patrimonio neto?
  • de Wie hoch ist mein Nettovermögen?
  • pt Qual é o meu patrimônio líquido?
  • ca Quin és el meu patrimoni net?

move_transactions

Move saved transactions onto another bank, cash wallet, or card that already exists. It does not copy them and does not log a new expense.

  • en Move those expenses from Swiss card to Cash.
  • es Mueve esos gastos de la tarjeta suiza a Efectivo.
  • de Verschiebe diese Ausgaben von der Swiss Card zu Bargeld.
  • pt Mova essas despesas do cartão suíço para Dinheiro.
  • ca Mou eixes gastos de la targeta suïssa a Efectiu.

delete_finance

Delete one account, transaction, recurring rule, budget, or budget line. Call first with confirmed false. kind is account, transaction, rule, budget, or budget_line. period is this or YYYY-MM for a budget. name or category selects a budget line. If an account has transactions, it says how many and can move them before removal. Waits for a clear yes.

  • en Delete Swiss card.
  • es Borra la tarjeta suiza.
  • de Lösche die Swiss Card.
  • pt Apague o cartão suíço.
  • ca Esborra la targeta suïssa.

save_recurring

Save a repeating bill, payday, subscription, card payment, or automated investment. cadence can be every N days, weeks, months, or years.

  • en Add a 20 franc payment every 10 days starting tomorrow.
  • es Añade un pago de 20 francos cada 10 días desde mañana.
  • de Füge eine Zahlung von 20 Franken alle 10 Tage ab morgen hinzu.
  • pt Adicione um pagamento de 20 francos a cada 10 dias a partir de amanhã.
  • ca Afig un pagament de 20 francs cada 10 dies a partir de demà.

save_schedule

Program a fixed run, open-ended rent, a subscription, a one-off, planned income, or an investment payout. It stays planned until mark_paid. Changing the amount applies to future unpaid occurrences.

  • en A course for 9 months at 270 euros.
  • es Un curso de 9 meses a 270 euros.
  • de Ein Kurs für 9 Monate zu 270 Euro.
  • pt Um curso de 9 meses a 270 euros.
  • ca Un curs de 9 mesos a 270 euros.

mark_paid

Mark a saved bill, rent, subscription, or card payment as paid. It logs that amount and moves a repeating date forward. confirmed is not required.

  • en Mark rent paid.
  • es Marca el alquiler como pagado.
  • de Markiere die Miete als bezahlt.
  • pt Marque o aluguel como pago.
  • ca Marca el lloguer com a pagat.

save_budget

Add, check, change, or remove the budget for a month. “Set restaurants to 400 euros this month” sets that limit, and saying it again changes the same line. “How much is left for restaurants?” reads it. “Read the budget” says income, spending, savings, and investments. “Am I over the plan?” says whether spending has gone past the plan. Two paydays can stay two lines, such as Salary 1 Nov and Salary 15 Nov. “Fill the budget” or “Set up the budget” fills the month from repeating bills and recent spending. A delete asks first. Nothing is removed until you say yes. Grok does not make up an amount.

  • en Set restaurants to 400 euros this month.
  • es Pon restaurantes en 400 euros este mes.
  • de Setze Restaurants diesen Monat auf 400 Euro.
  • pt Defina restaurantes em 400 euros este mês.
  • ca Posa restaurants a 400 euros este mes.

open_finance

Open a Finance screen and answer with the same numbers you see. Plan is the month’s budget. Repeating bills stay in Activity. “Describe the money flow” says income, spending, and debt payments. A card charge is spending. Paying a card or a loan is separate. A color you set is used on every chart that shows that item. Clearing every color asks first.

  • en Show my holdings chart.
  • es Muestra el gráfico de posiciones.
  • de Zeig das Bestandsdiagramm.
  • pt Mostre o gráfico de posições.
  • ca Mostra el gràfic de posicions.

export_finance_pdf

Write a Finance PDF on this phone and open the share sheet. Call first with confirmed false, say the period, then call with confirmed true. Nothing is uploaded. Waits for a clear yes.

  • en Export this month’s finance PDF.
  • es Exporta el PDF de finanzas de este mes.
  • de Exportiere das Finanz-PDF dieses Monats.
  • pt Exporte o PDF de finanças deste mês.
  • ca Exporta el PDF de finances d’este mes.

Save a backup of the data kept on this phone, including pinned facts and API keys, and open the share sheet. Talk history and voice-time counters are not in the file. The voice file is not encrypted. Talk will not take a passphrase. Say that the file contains keys.

Replace everything on this phone with the newest backup saved here. The first call uses confirmed false and does not restore. Say what will be replaced, wait for yes, then call again with confirmed true. An encrypted file is restored in Settings, where you type the passphrase. Waits for a clear yes.

  • en Restore my backup.

  • pt Restaura a minha cópia.

It refuses API keys, backup passwords, and reordering voice keys. language is English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Valencian, or follow the phone.

  • en Switch the language to Spanish.
  • es Cambia el idioma a español.
  • de Stelle die Sprache auf Spanisch.
  • pt Mude o idioma para espanhol.
  • ca Canvia la llengua a espanyol.
