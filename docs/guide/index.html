Install it, set it up, then talk.

This guide matches Grok Voice 0.1.65. Talk shows chat bubbles. Finance Activity is Transactions and Recurring. Plan is a month budget. A short landscape screen uses a side rail. The commands are the tools the app actually exposes, not a guessed list.

Finance, Talk, and landscape

Your lines in Talk sit on the right in an accent bubble. Grok sits on the left on a neutral surface. A bubble stays within about 80% of the width, with a small tail. There is no role label.

Finance tabs are Overview, Activity, Accounts, Holdings, and Plan. Activity splits into Transactions and Recurring. The same filters, including Income, apply to both. A recurring rule with no saved amount shows the card’s statement amount or the balance due, and the row says which. Holdings shares of priced holdings add up to 100%. The portfolio value row reads 100% and is not counted again in that sum.

The money-flow chart counts a card charge as spending. Paying a card or a loan is a separate Debt payments flow, so spending matches Activity expenses for the period. A line under the chart states income, expenses, and debt payments. Still owed lists unpaid card and loan balances and does not add them to the period. The legend uses the chart colors. On a short landscape screen the chart stays in its column and the legend sits beside it, in two columns.

Plan is a budget for one month. Recurring bills stay in Activity. The month can move 24 months back and 12 ahead. You see expected income, each expense limit next to what was spent, what is left to spend, and a savings target. An empty month can start from recurring rules, from recent months, or from both. Talk can create, read, update, and delete that budget. A delete waits for a clear yes. Filling a month runs only when you ask to set up or fill the budget.

On a short landscape phone, about 844 by 390, the subtitle is hidden, the title stays with the tabs, and the bottom bar becomes a side rail. The add action folds into the rail. Plan puts the summary on the left and the category lines on the right.
