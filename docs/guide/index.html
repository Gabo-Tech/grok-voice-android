Talk, money, and landscape

What you say appears in a rounded bubble on the right, in the accent color, with a small tail. Grok’s reply sits on the left.

Finance is Overview, Activity, Accounts, Holdings, and Plan. Activity keeps transactions and repeating bills apart, and the filters, including Income, apply to both. If a repeating payment has no amount of its own, the row shows the card statement or the balance due, and says which. In Holdings, the shares add up to 100%.

On the money-flow chart, a card charge is spending. Paying a card or a loan is separate, so it is not counted as spending again. Under the chart you see income, expenses, and debt payments. What you still owe is listed on its own. The legend uses the same colors as the chart, and in landscape it sits beside the chart.

Plan is a budget for one month. Repeating bills stay in Activity. You can look 24 months back and 12 ahead. You see expected income, each limit next to what you already spent, what is left, and a savings target. An empty month can start from repeating bills, from recent months, or from both. You can ask Talk to add, check, change, or remove that budget. A delete waits until you say yes. “Fill the budget” or “Set up the budget” fills the month from those bills and recent spending.

In landscape, navigation moves to a slim side rail, and Add sits there too. On Plan, the summary is on the left and the categories are on the right.
